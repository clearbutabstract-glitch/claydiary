Meet the Maker

Hi, I'm Mia Calixto — a Brooklyn-born, self-taught potter.

I never went to art school for ceramics. Everything I know about clay I picked up by watching the techs and instructors at my studio, getting my hands dirty, and teaching kids' after-school classes since 2019. Watching a kid see their idea come to life in clay for the first time — that joy is the whole point.

Today I make functional, hand-thrown stoneware and porcelain out of my Brooklyn studio. I'm also a studio tech, a ceramics teacher for kids, and a college student studying Communication Design. When I'm not at the wheel, I'm painting, taking photos, or experimenting with mixed media.

Clay Diary is where I share the work — and the messy, joyful process behind it.
